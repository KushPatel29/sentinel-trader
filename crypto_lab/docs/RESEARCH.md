Research reviewed 15 November 2026. This is a focused review of primary sources, not a claim to have read every trading repository or all market knowledge.

The licenses in this review are contextual observations, not a license audit. This implementation is original; it does not bundle these frameworks.

**What the first experiment measures.** BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT spot candles, every four hours, 2021-01-01 to 2026-11-15 04:00 UTC. These coins were selected today, so the universe is subject to survivorship bias. Features cover returns, moving-average distances, volatility, RSI, candle shape, volume, buyer-initiated volume imbalance, drawdowns, crowding proxies and time of week. Price proxies are not direct measurements of psychology. No claim is made that all market drivers are represented.

**Selection.** After 200 days of feature warmup, use 540 days for training and 180 days for validation; test the chosen candidate on the next 90 days. The last 180 days are a separate frozen test with one pre-boundary selection and no refit inside it. For ML, fit on the first 80% of the training block, calibrate on the last 20%, and purge seven four-hour rows around forward labels. A classifier forecasts the open-to-open direction over six future bars (24 hours), starting at the next open. A model's accuracy is measured on non-overlapping label samples and compared with its pre-test class/base-rate prediction.

**Objective.** Cash is always available. A candidate needs at least four validation round trips, validation Sharpe above 0.5, positive validation returns after doubled costs, and validation drawdown under 15%. Among eligible candidates, rank stress Sharpe plus twice the negative drawdown. This rule was fixed before the test. It does not imply statistically proven profitability.

**Execution and risk.** Long or cash only; no margin, shorts, perpetuals or funding. Three independent initial capital sleeves, each capped at 60% exposure. Target annual volatility is 20%, scaled down in 10-percentage-point steps. Rebalance changes below 2.5% of sleeve equity are ignored. Historical decisions execute at the next open, with 10 bps fee and 5 bps slippage per side; positions are liquidated at test boundaries with costs. Fees are configurable assumptions, not the user's verified fee tier. Baselines pay the same costs. The 2% UTC daily-loss and 15% sleeve drawdown circuit breakers act at observed prices; they cannot guarantee a loss cap through gaps or outages.

**Forward paper experiment.** Each candidate and the validation-selected policy gets an independent $10,000 simulated portfolio across the three sleeves. All portfolios are shadow experiments, not additive capital. The worker starts in cash and waits for a future completed bar. It uses subsequently observed bid/ask, additional slippage and fees. A late signal or downtime never creates a historical fill. Sixty-second quote checks make forward risk exits more frequent than the historical four-hour close/open checks; report this simulation difference rather than imply identical fills. Policy/model parameters stay fixed during the forward experiment. Retraining is an explicit new prospective version, not an automatic response to losing money.

**What remains.** At least 60 days of new forward observations, costs calibrated to the intended venue and order size, fee sensitivity beyond the 2x scenario, independent-venue replication, delisted-coin/universe controls, longer untouched future tests, and timestamped macro/news/on-chain ablation studies. A bigger neural network or more indicators is not a substitute for these checks. Even passing them would not guarantee profit.

**US stocks and commodities.** The repository's original equity files are retained. `src/sentinel/data` and `src/sentinel/models` were absent in the supplied folder, ZIP and initial Git commit, and were restored by upstream commit `5faadfa` while this work was in progress. Those updates and the subsequent cloud fixes were integrated before this crypto addition. The old reported trading results have not been reproduced in this experiment. Adjusted prices, dividends, calendars and feed consistency require separate stock testing. [Alpaca's data documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/historical-stock-data-1) distinguishes the single-exchange IEX feed from consolidated SIP data. Futures also require contract rolls and margin/expiry handling; a continuous price chart is insufficient. This crypto experiment makes no new stock or commodity performance claim.
